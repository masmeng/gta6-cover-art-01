**Vice Cover Studio** turns your photos into GTA-style cover art: a comic-book poster with several photo panels, a sunset or neon colour grade, halftone dots, and a big bold caption — exported as a share-ready PNG.

## How it works

1. **Photos** — click a built-in sample or upload your own. Click a photo, then click a panel on the cover to place it there. Drag on the cover to move a photo inside its panel; the zoom slider frames it.
2. **Layout** — Poster (one hero panel over two), Grid (2 × 2) or Strips (three vertical panels).
3. **Grade** — Sunset (warm pink-orange), Neon (cyan-magenta nights) or Noir (high-contrast black and white), plus optional comic halftone dots.
4. **Caption** — a title in heavy display type with a retro offset shadow, and a letterspaced subtitle.
5. **Size** — 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. **Export PNG** downloads the cover at full size. Shuffle rolls a random layout, grade and photos.

Everything runs offline in a desktop browser — no network requests, no uploads, photos never leave your machine. The four sample photos are original images made with an image model, and the display font is Anton (SIL Open Font License, see `app/fonts/OFL.txt`). No game logos, artwork or fonts are used.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
